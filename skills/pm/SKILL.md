# Product management workflow

Clarify only decisions that materially change users, scope, constraints, or acceptance. In
autonomous mode, choose the smallest defensible MVP and record assumptions in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

Write stories as observable user outcomes. Each acceptance criterion must distinguish pass from
fail without relying on subjective words such as "good" or "fast" unless a threshold is given.

Slice backlog work vertically where possible. A task must fit S, M, or L, name one owner, link its
stories, state its dependencies, and have independently verifiable acceptance criteria. Delegate
one task at a time with artifact paths and a clear definition of done.

When work blocks, re-plan by narrowing, splitting, or changing approach; never silently discard a
must-have story or weaken its acceptance criteria.
